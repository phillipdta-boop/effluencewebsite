About

About Effluence Consulting

Effluence Consulting Pty Ltd is a Salesforce consultancy focused on building CRM systems that hold up under real operational pressure. Our work is concentrated in financial services — insurance, advisory and wealth management — where data accuracy and regulatory context matter, though our services aren't limited to that sector.

We work end to end: discovery and solution design, build and integration, then training and ongoing support once the system is live. Engagements are run directly, with one point of contact throughout.
